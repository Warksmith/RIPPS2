[SIZE=5][B]RIPPS2 v1.5.7 beta: BIBLE a060790 inside, a ripped CD track from an SD2PSX, Warksmith's and RiptOPL's fixes[/B][/SIZE]
[URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/latest/download/RIPPS2.elf']RIPPS2.elf[/URL] (2,780 KB)  |  [URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/releases/tag/v1.5.7-beta']What's new[/URL]  |  [URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md']Changelog[/URL][/CENTER]

Everything since 1.4.1: builds 142 to 157. In the PCSX2 lab it boots, starts a game from an exFAT HDD on the built-in core, and walks Memory Files; the new parts below have not been on a real PS2 yet, so your reports count.

[B]BIBLE a060790 inside.[/B] If your card has BIBLE, Manage offers the update:
[LIST]
[*][B]One settings file on a card.[/B] BIBLE reads PS2BBL.INI from this console's region folder first, then the other regions', then SYS-CONF. A card that boots consoles of several regions keeps one copy.
[*][B]The AUTO hang is fixed.[/B] A missing file on an AUTO path, on a drive BIBLE loads a driver for, no longer leaves BIBLE on its splash. It moves on to the next path (passed on a SCPH-39001 with an SD2PSX).
[*][B]BIBLE no longer reads the boot sound into memory.[/B] RIPPS2 reads it, and the towers come about 0.8 s sooner.
[*]ROM 2.30 and up: Manage warns that a bound BIBLE may not start at power on there. BIBLE's card icon is remade.
[/LIST]

[B]A ripped CD track plays from an SD2PSX.[/B] The music was read in long runs that held the card's bus while the sound driver had only a tenth of a second queued, so a .wav (nine times an Ogg's data) skipped. RIPPS2 now reads it in small pieces with a short gap after each on MMCE. Keep .wav for your rips; for music kept on MMCE, .ogg at 44.1 kHz stereo is the safe choice. DIAG.TXT now logs each track's read speed.

[B]From Warksmith:[/B] UDPFS with OPL and plain libraries on one share, disc IDs checked once and kept; in-game return detects a packed Neutrino, the pad hook recovers, and MMCE cards are put back on the way out.

[B]From RiptOPL:[/B] MX4SIO saves no longer lose sectors (the fixed driver from ps2sdk #936 is built in), two IOP hangs in the CD stream emulation are gone, .hack//Infection's IOP allocations fit, and 8-bit art keeps its colours.

[B]Fixes:[/B] Save Settings To stays where you chose (thanks nuno6573), All Games keeps every drive, the internal HDD comes back after an IOP restart (no more 401 on exFAT), RIPSHOT on MMCE makes its roll, Back on a changed page asks first, a colour picker for every colour row, one Controller Settings page with a Button Map.

Tell us what breaks here or [URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/issues']open an issue[/URL], with your console model, ROM, what you did and your DIAG.TXT.
