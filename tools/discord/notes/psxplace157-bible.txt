[B]BIBLE has one release now, and it rolls.[/B] The links above always give the newest signed build, so the old release links in this thread are gone: use these.

[LIST]
[*][B]One settings file on a card.[/B] BIBLE reads PS2BBL.INI from this console's region folder first (mc?:/B?EXEC-SYSTEM), then the other regions', then SYS-CONF for a card set up before. A card that boots consoles of several regions keeps one copy, with boot.adp beside it.
[*][B]The AUTO hang is fixed[/B] (from f5705eb): a missing file on an AUTO path, on a drive BIBLE loads a driver for, no longer leaves BIBLE on its splash. It moves on to the next path. That passed on a SCPH-39001 with an SD2PSX.
[*][B]BIBLE no longer reads the boot sound into memory.[/B] It tells RIPPS2 the folder and RIPPS2 reads it: about 0.8 s sooner to the towers in the lab.
[*][B]A new card icon[/B], remade in Blender, its PS2 logo in the splash's gradient.
[/LIST]

[B]BIBLE's Settings in RIPPS2 1.5.7[/B]: every setting with a tip, [B]BOOT LOCK[/B] (RIPPS2 first at power on, and a game's EXIT back the same way), [B]Other Regions[/B] (BIBLE for other regions' consoles on the same card, each region's folder holding only its boot file) and [B]Launch Keys[/B]. Sky Color opens a colour picker for the sky's top, horizon and floor.

[B]With RIPPS2 1.5.7, nothing to download:[/B] Settings > General > Boot Loader > Manage installs a060790 the one-folder way and offers the update to a card with 3282396 or f5705eb. For KELFBinder, BIBLE-signed.zip; for consoles with no system update (many from ROM 2.30 on), BIBLE-ELF.zip and OpenTuna or FunTuna.

a060790 itself has not been bound on a console yet. Tell us here or [URL='https://github.com/akilluminati47/RIPPS2/issues']open an issue[/URL] with your console model, ROM and card, PAL and Japanese consoles most of all.
